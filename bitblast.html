BitBlast: The Artillery War

Tank movement, terrain collision, and missile behavior for an artillery game.

BitBlast

The browser build is pending. These controls apply to the original Windows version.

Controls

A D
Move left and right
W S
Raise and lower the turret
Q E
Decrease and increase shot power
Space
Fire the selected missile
Left Shift
Rocket jump
1–5
Select Standard, Cluster, Sticky Bomb, Rain, or Bounce
T Enter
Open multiplayer chat and send a message
Esc
Close chat or open the in-game menu

My contribution

I used axis-aligned bounding boxes for collision checks and a state machine for idle, movement, and jumping. I also implemented per-weapon ammo tracking and the bounce missile.
