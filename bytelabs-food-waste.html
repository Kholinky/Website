ByteLabs – Food Waste

A web app that connects stores with organizations that can collect and redistribute surplus food.

View on Devpost

Demo video

Watch on YouTube

About the app

Stores list surplus food and its availability. Organizations can reserve pickup slots and track collections, while daily item limits help distribute food across participants. The prototype uses React and a Node.js/Express API, with separate access for stores, organizations, and admins.

How food gets redistributed

Surplus listings

Stores publish available items, and organizations can see what is available as listings change.

Pickup booking

Organizations reserve a collection slot. The booking flow was designed to avoid scheduling conflicts and applies daily item limits.

Collection tracking

An organization dashboard keeps track of bookings and pickups. Stores, organizations, and admins have separate access.
