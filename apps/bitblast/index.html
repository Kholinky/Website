Play BitBlast

A browser adaptation of the original turn-based artillery game.

Game display

Online two-player
  1. Player 1 creates an offer and sends the code to Player 2 privately.
  2. Player 2 pastes the offer, selects Answer offer, then sends the generated answer back.
  3. Player 1 pastes that answer and selects Connect.

Online play uses a direct peer connection. Some school, office, or carrier networks may block it.
