Is It Real

A deepfake detector that explains why it flags an image and accepts user feedback.

View on Devpost

About the app

The app combines machine learning with computer vision checks for signs of manipulation, including unusual compression patterns and inconsistent lighting. The Streamlit interface shows the evidence behind a verdict and lets users submit corrections. The project also supports sampling frames from video.

How the detector works

Analyze an image or video

Upload an image or video in Streamlit. For video, the app samples frames for analysis.

Explain the verdict

The detector combines model predictions with forensic cues such as compression artifacts, unusual frequency patterns, and inconsistent lighting. The interface shows the evidence and confidence behind a result.

Learn from feedback

Users can mark a verdict as correct or wrong. The prototype uses that feedback to update its model and stores versioned checkpoints on Hugging Face.
