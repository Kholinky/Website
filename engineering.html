Tengah sustainable estate

Major Project · 2021

For my final-year team project, we proposed a sustainable estate in Tengah. We took inspiration from HDB’s plans for a town set in nature and used Revit to design around existing trees, leaving room for greenery. I used Enscape to render the final model and show how the buildings and landscaping would work together.

Garden Fiesta redesign

Sustainable Design Project · 2021

I analyzed Block 32A at Temasek Polytechnic, including its surroundings and the strengths and weaknesses of the existing building. My proposal included a new floor plan, a 3D massing study with vertical greenery on each façade, and a revised road layout based on typical Singapore road dimensions.

I used simulations to assess the sustainability measures and documented the final proposal on an A1 presentation board.

Green building modeling and simulation

Building performance study · 2020

My teammate and I used solar and shadow studies to see which parts of our building received more or less heat. We used computational fluid dynamics to visualize wind speed and flow around the building and identify changes to the design. The work combined building modeling with performance analysis in BimHvac.

Engineering experience

During my ES Management internship, I carried out technical audits at 71 MOE primary and secondary schools and worked with facility managers and principals on BCA Green Mark certification. The internship formed part of my broader engineering experience with the company.
